# SignalTuner Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 12, 2026**  
**Last Updated: August 12, 2026**

SignalTuner Inc. (“SignalTuner,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you use SignalTuner, including the SignalTuner Microsoft Teams application, SignalTuner websites, desktop applications, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

SignalTuner is primarily intended for business and professional use. The Service may be used by individual users as well as users whose accounts are associated with an employer, organization, or Microsoft Teams environment.

By using SignalTuner, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use SignalTuner.

### Account Information

When you create or use a SignalTuner account, we may collect:

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Password hash when email/password authentication is used
- Microsoft or Microsoft Teams account identifiers when Microsoft Teams Single Sign-On (“Teams SSO”) is used
- SignalTuner activation codes used to associate a desktop client with your account

Passwords are not stored in plain text.

### Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Account Information

If you use SignalTuner through Microsoft Teams or authenticate using Teams SSO, SignalTuner may receive information made available by Microsoft based on the permissions granted to SignalTuner, the Microsoft Teams context in which SignalTuner is used, and your organization's Microsoft configuration.

This information may include, as applicable:

- Microsoft account or user identifiers
- Display name and other profile information
- Microsoft tenant or organization identifiers
- Meeting identifiers
- Meeting participant names or identifiers
- Organization or tenant context
- Information necessary to identify and display participants within a Teams meeting
- Other meeting-context information that Microsoft makes available to SignalTuner through authorized APIs or the Teams application environment

The specific information accessible to SignalTuner may vary based on Microsoft's APIs, permissions, user configuration, administrator configuration, and changes Microsoft makes to the Teams platform.

SignalTuner does not use Microsoft Teams data for advertising.

### Desktop Client and Connectivity Telemetry

SignalTuner provides a desktop client that users may download and activate. The desktop client collects technical telemetry for the purpose of identifying and resolving connectivity, network, and system performance issues.

Telemetry may include:

- Wi-Fi signal strength
- Wi-Fi network or SSID
- Wi-Fi band
- Current network information
- Download speed
- Upload speed
- Network latency or ping
- Packet loss
- CPU usage
- Memory usage
- Processor information
- Processor core information
- VPN detection or status
- Network adapter information
- Device identifier or UUID
- SignalTuner Signal Score
- Connectivity or diagnostic status
- Online or offline status
- Related network and system diagnostic measurements

The desktop client uses an activation code to associate this telemetry with a SignalTuner account.

This telemetry is primarily technical diagnostic information rather than communications content. However, because certain telemetry may contain persistent device or network identifiers and may be associated with a SignalTuner account, we treat it as personal information where required by applicable law.

SignalTuner does not use desktop telemetry to determine your geographic location.

### Device Identifiers

SignalTuner may create, receive, or store persistent device identifiers, machine identifiers, UUIDs, or similar identifiers to distinguish devices and associate telemetry with the appropriate account or device.

### Payment Information

If you purchase a paid SignalTuner product or subscription, payment transactions may be processed by Stripe or another third-party payment processor.

SignalTuner does **not** store full payment-card numbers or banking credentials.

Payment processors may collect payment information directly from you and process that information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. SignalTuner may receive transaction-related information such as payment status, subscription status, product or plan information, customer identifiers, and transaction identifiers.

### Email and Communications Information

SignalTuner uses Brevo and may use similar communications providers to send transactional, account-related, service-related, or marketing emails.

SignalTuner may provide Brevo with:

- Your name
- Your email address

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe mechanism included in those communications. You may continue to receive transactional or service-related communications that are necessary to operate your account or provide the Service.

### Cookies

SignalTuner uses cookies or similar technologies for authentication and session management.

We do not currently use cookies for:

- Advertising
- Cross-site behavioral tracking
- Cross-context behavioral advertising

SignalTuner may use analytics technologies as described below.

### Analytics Information

SignalTuner may use Google Analytics or other analytics providers to understand how users interact with the Service, diagnose performance issues, measure usage, and improve SignalTuner.

Analytics providers may process technical information such as browser type, device type, pages or features used, timestamps, session information, and general usage information.

SignalTuner does not use analytics services for targeted advertising or cross-site behavioral advertising.

If applicable law requires consent, an opt-out mechanism, or another control for a particular analytics technology, SignalTuner will provide the required mechanism.

### Location Information

SignalTuner does not intentionally collect precise or approximate geographic location information and does not use IP addresses to determine your geographic location.

As with most Internet services, hosting providers and network infrastructure may necessarily process an IP address temporarily in connection with routing communications, security, or operation of the Service.

## 3. Sharing Telemetry With Meeting Participants

A core purpose of SignalTuner is to help participants identify and resolve connectivity problems during online meetings.

When SignalTuner is used in a Microsoft Teams meeting, connectivity and diagnostic telemetry associated with participating SignalTuner users may be displayed to **other participants in that meeting**.

Information displayed may include technical measurements such as:

- Connectivity status
- Signal Score
- Wi-Fi performance
- Network performance
- Latency
- Packet loss
- Bandwidth measurements
- Device or system-performance indicators
- Other diagnostic information necessary to identify connectivity problems

This information is shared for the purpose of diagnosing and resolving meeting connectivity or performance problems.

SignalTuner does not sell this telemetry to meeting participants or use this sharing for advertising.

Users should understand that diagnostic information displayed through SignalTuner during a meeting may be visible to other participants in that meeting.

## 6. Data Retention

SignalTuner retains different categories of information for different periods.

### Live Telemetry

Current or live telemetry is maintained while the user's SignalTuner desktop client is active and reporting information.

When the client is no longer active, current telemetry ceases to represent an active diagnostic session.

### Historical Telemetry and Reports

Historical telemetry and diagnostic reports are generally retained for up to **30 days**, after which they are deleted or overwritten unless a longer period is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

### Meeting-Related Information

SignalTuner does not maintain a separate historical record of meeting-related telemetry merely because telemetry was displayed within a meeting.

Telemetry that also forms part of a user's ordinary diagnostic history remains subject to the 30-day retention period described above.

### Account Deletion

Users may delete their SignalTuner account through the Service.

Upon account deletion, account records are deleted from SignalTuner's active systems. Some information may temporarily remain in routine system backups, security records, transaction records, or third-party service-provider systems where retention is required for operational, security, financial, or legal purposes.

Information that SignalTuner is legally required to retain may be retained for the required period.
